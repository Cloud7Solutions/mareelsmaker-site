MaReelMaker

MaReelMaker is a personal macOS app that turns a music track and a set of photographs into a video reel: each photo is shown in a classic gallery frame on a softly lit wall while the camera glides from picture to picture, for exactly the length of the music.

What it does

  • Finds high-quality photos by subject and mood from free stock-photo services, credited to their photographers.
  • Renders the reel on your Mac and exports an MP4 in common social-media formats (vertical, square and landscape).
  • Optionally uploads a finished video to your own YouTube channel, after you sign in with Google and choose the channel.

Your Google and YouTube data

MaReelMaker uses Google sign-in only to upload videos you choose to your YouTube channel and to show which channel is connected. Everything runs on your own Mac; nothing is sent to the developer. See the privacy policy for details.
